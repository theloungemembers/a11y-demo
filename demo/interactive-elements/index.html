Buttons vs Divs

Native Button

Natively receives tab focus, responds to Space and Enter keys, and is announced as a 'Button'.

<button>

Fake Button (Div)

Divs do not receive focus, do not respond to keyboard events (Space/Enter), and do not tell screen readers they are interactive.

<div onClick={}>
Fake Button

Patched Fake Button

If you MUST use a div (you shouldn't), you have to manually add role, tabIndex, and keyboard event listeners. It's error-prone.
