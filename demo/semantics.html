Heading Hierarchy

Correct Hierarchy

Headings follow a logical, sequential order (h1 -> h2 -> h3).

Sequential Headings

Section Heading (h2)

Content describing the section.

Subsection (h3)

Details for the subsection.

Sub-subsection (h4)

Granular details.

Incorrect Hierarchy

Headings skip levels, confusing screen reader navigation.

Section Heading (h2)

Content describing the section.
